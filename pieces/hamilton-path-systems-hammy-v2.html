Technical Notes

HOW SUB-STACKS WORK

For complex reasoning, enable fractal mode on Verify and Constraint nodes. Verify runs its own 5-1-5 to check hallucinations: Why=grounding, Context=retrieved docs, Constraint=truth threshold. Each sub-inference logs separately, creating a merkle-tree of accountability.

TRADEOFFS: LATENCY VS ACCOUNTABILITY
Fractal
  • + Full trace per decision
  • + Debuggable failures
  • - 50-500ms × depth
  • - 10-100× compute
Flat
  • + <100ms p95
  • + Cheap at scale
  • - Black-box internals
  • - Weaker audit trail
RECOMMENDATION

Most use cases: Start Flat. Enable Fractal selectively (depth=2 max) on Verify, Constraint, and Human nodes for high-risk domains (medical, finance, legal). Use sampling: fractal 1% of traffic for continuous audit. Cache sub-stack results by context-hash.
